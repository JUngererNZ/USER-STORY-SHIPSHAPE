### **User Story: Standardized Operational Handoff for CN1s**

**As an** FML-OPS Coordinator,  
**I want** to capture and validate CN1-related shipment data within a dedicated Shipshape digital form,  
**So that** I can instantly hand off complete, accurate records to Cargo Services without the need for manual email attachments or "email ping-pong" correspondence.

---

### **Acceptance Criteria (FML-OPS Perspective)**
* **[ ] Automated Data Capture:** The form must automatically pull operational data (Consignee, Commodity, etc.) directly from existing Shipshape files to prevent manual re-typing.
* **[ ] Digital Repository Link:** Ability to link all relevant 5x PDF documents directly to the digital record within Shipshape, eliminating the need to attach them to outbound emails.
* **[ ] Integrated Handoff Trigger:** A "Submit to CS" or "Ready for Clearance" button that notifies Cargo Services the data is ready for their final input.
* **[ ] Submission Log:** A timestamped audit log that shows exactly when the data was handed over to CS, ensuring accountability for the processing timeline.
* **[ ] Field Validation:** The system must highlight missing "Operations" fields before allowing the handoff, ensuring Cargo Services receives a 100% complete draft form.
